Goodness me, it's been a busy month. So, little bit of personal background, I've been helping out at this local pizzeria on & off.

And like, it's a lovely place, and the owners are very good to me, but good god does the place stress me out. 

When I finished up working at the lab, I needed something to pay the bills, so I went back to the pizza place, and wouldn't you know it I'm the most senior person there. So I very quickly begin stepping up and saying hey, guys, what we're doing right now isn't okay. This isn't sustainable to your staff and you're bleeding customers - regulars, from before they even bought the place.

Now, I had zero intention of staying long. I figured I'd update my CV and get back into an admin gig, and with my spark finally back to get myself in tech I'd keep hustling towards the mythical big-girl-job.

Unfortunately, my incessant drive to organize and fix what I deem broken has quickly made me not just a voice of authority, but THE voice of authority at said hellish pizza place.

And thus, I am now a manager!

A very, painfully stressed out manager.

I'm trying to get back into building things. I studied all this, and it's so frustrating to have all my brainpower being used on herding cats (waiters & kitchen porters & chefs, oh my!) when I'm well aware of how capable I am when my creativity is able to flourish.

And so, this little site is the beginning of that. And next, a portfolio site, and after that perhaps my unfinished projects get a little love, and my cvs, and I start talking to folks again, and my linkedin gets updated, and you see me networking again....

I do very much enjoy the pomp & circumstance of networking, even if applying for positions in the job market is unusually soul-crushing. 

Still, it will most certainly be preferable to the stress of **Pizza Hell**. & if nothing else, I'll always know where to go for a little taste of Napoli.

All the best,
Eva
